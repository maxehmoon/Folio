# Code of Conduct

Folio's community should be a respectful place to contribute, ask questions and review work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions that breach these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious or repeated concerns should be reported privately to a maintainer rather than debated publicly.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issue trackers, code review and other spaces represented as part of the Folio project.
